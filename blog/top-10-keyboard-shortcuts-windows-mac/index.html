The three most people don’t know

Win + V (clipboard history). Windows remembers the last things you copied, not just the latest one. Press Win + V and pick any of them. The first time, Windows asks you to turn it on; say yes.

Win + Shift + S (screenshot an area). Drag a box around anything on screen and it’s on your clipboard, ready to paste into an email or chat. On Mac, ⌘ + ⇧ + 4 does the same; add Ctrl to copy instead of saving a file.

⌘ + Space (Spotlight). On a Mac, stop hunting through the Dock. Press it, type the first few letters of any app or file, and hit Return.

How to make them stick

Don’t try to learn all ten at once. Pick one today and force yourself to use it every time, even when the mouse is faster for the first few tries. When it’s automatic (usually a few days), add the next one. In a month your hands will barely leave the keyboard.
