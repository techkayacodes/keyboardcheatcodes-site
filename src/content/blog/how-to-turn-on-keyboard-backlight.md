Typing in the dark and can't see the keys? Most laptops have a backlight; it's just hidden behind a key combination. Look for a small **keyboard-with-rays icon** on one of your keys: that's the one to press.

## Lenovo ThinkPad

On most ThinkPads, hold **Fn** and press the **Space bar**. Each press cycles through off, low and high. The short above shows it on my ThinkPad.

## Dell

On most Dell laptops it's **F10**, **F5** or **Fn + F10** (look for the backlight icon). Press it again to change the brightness.

## HP

On most HP laptops it's **F5**, **F9** or **F11**, sometimes with **Fn**. Look for the icon with three dots and a line.

## MacBook

Newer MacBooks adjust the backlight automatically. To change it yourself, open **Control Center** in the menu bar and use **Keyboard Brightness**. Older models have dedicated brightness keys on **F5** and **F6**.

## Nothing happens?

1. **Check for the icon.** If no key has a backlight symbol, your model may not have a backlight.
2. **Try with and without Fn.** Some laptops swap the function keys, so you need Fn for one and not the other.
3. **Check the manufacturer's app.** Lenovo Vantage, Dell's keyboard settings and HP's tools can turn the backlight on and set a timeout.
4. **Wait, then tap a key.** Many backlights switch off after a few seconds without typing and come back when you press a key.
