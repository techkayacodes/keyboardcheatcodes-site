Most slow typing isn't about talent. It's a few habits you picked up without noticing. Here are the seven I see most, with a fix for each.

## 1. Using the wrong fingers for keys

If your index fingers do all the work, you've hit a ceiling. **Fix:** rest your fingers on the home row (ASDF and JKL;) and give each finger its own column of keys. It feels slower for a week, then it's much faster for good.

## 2. Not using the Shift keys properly

Reaching for left Shift with your left hand to type a capital *A* twists your hand. **Fix:** use the Shift key on the *opposite* side of the letter you're capitalizing.

## 3. Overusing Backspace

Tapping Backspace eight times to fix one word wastes seconds every minute. **Fix:** delete whole words at once with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Backspace</kbd> (<kbd>⌥</kbd> + <kbd>Delete</kbd> on Mac). Here's the [full guide to deleting a whole word](/shortcuts/delete-whole-word/).

## 4. Typing with flat fingers

Flat fingers drag across the keys and hit their neighbors. **Fix:** curve your fingers slightly, like you're holding a ball, and strike with the fingertips.

## 5. Looking at the keyboard

Every glance down breaks your rhythm and makes you lose your place on screen. **Fix:** find the little bumps on F and J with your index fingers, and keep your eyes on the screen even when it means more typos at first.

## 6. Not using keyboard shortcuts

Reaching for the mouse to copy, paste, switch windows or close a tab costs a few seconds every time, hundreds of times a day. **Fix:** learn one shortcut a week. The [shortcut library](/shortcuts/) has a quick guide for each.

## 7. Not practicing regularly

Speed comes from repetition. **Fix:** ten minutes a day on a free typing site beats one long session a month.

## Quick wins for speed and accuracy

- **Learn touch typing** properly, even if it slows you down at first.
- **Sit up straight** with your wrists floating, not resting on the desk edge.
- **Use a comfortable keyboard.** See [10 types of keyboards](/blog/10-types-of-keyboards/) if yours feels like a wet sponge.
- **Take breaks.** Tired hands make mistakes.
- **Use a typing program** to track your words per minute as you improve.
