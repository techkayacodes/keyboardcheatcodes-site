Go full screen

Puts the current app in full-screen mode, hiding the taskbar, toolbars and everything else.

WindowsF11
Mac⌃+⌘+F

How to use it

  1. Click the app.
  2. Press the shortcut.
  3. Press it again (or Esc on Windows) to leave full screen.

Pro tip

Great for reading, presentations and focus. In browsers it hides the tabs and address bar too.

Heads up: F11 works in browsers and File Explorer on Windows; not every app supports it. On some laptops, press Fn + F11.
