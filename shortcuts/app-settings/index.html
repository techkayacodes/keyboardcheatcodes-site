Open the current app's settings

On a Mac, opens Settings (Preferences) for whatever app you're using.

WindowsNo built-in shortcut
Mac⌘+,

How to use it

  1. Click the app.
  2. Press ⌘ + , (comma).
  3. Its settings window opens.

Pro tip

Lots of Windows apps borrow this one too: Ctrl + , opens settings in VS Code, Slack, Discord and Teams.
