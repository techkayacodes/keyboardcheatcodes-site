Select one word at a time

Selects text a word at a time, which is much more accurate than dragging the mouse.

WindowsCtrl+Shift+← / →
Mac⌥+⇧+← / →

How to use it

  1. Put the cursor at the start of what you want.
  2. Hold Ctrl + Shift (Option + Shift on Mac).
  3. Tap the arrow keys to grow the selection word by word.

Pro tip

Overshot? Keep holding the keys and tap the opposite arrow to shrink the selection.
