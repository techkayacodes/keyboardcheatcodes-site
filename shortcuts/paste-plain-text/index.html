Paste as plain text

Pastes just the words, without the fonts, colors and links from wherever you copied them.

WindowsCtrl+Shift+V
Mac⌥+⇧+⌘+V

How to use it

  1. Copy text from a website or email.
  2. Click into your document or message.
  3. Press the shortcut to paste it in the surrounding style.

Pro tip

Works in Chrome, Edge, Google Docs, Slack, Teams and most modern apps.

Heads up: In Microsoft Word, Ctrl + Shift + V pastes formatting instead. Use Paste Options > Keep Text Only there.
