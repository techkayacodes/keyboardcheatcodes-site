Hide an app

On a Mac, hides every window of the current app instantly. It keeps running; it's just out of sight.

WindowsNo built-in shortcut
Mac⌘+H

How to use it

  1. Click the app.
  2. Press ⌘ + H.
  3. Switch back to it with ⌘ + Tab and its windows return.

Pro tip

⌥ + ⌘ + H hides every app except the one you're using. Instant focus mode.
