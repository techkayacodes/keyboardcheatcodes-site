Show or hide the Dock

On a Mac, turns Dock auto-hide on or off, so you get the full height of your screen back.

WindowsNo built-in shortcut
Mac⌥+⌘+D

How to use it

  1. Press ⌥ + ⌘ + D.
  2. The Dock slides away.
  3. Move the pointer to the bottom of the screen to bring it back, or press the shortcut again.

Pro tip

Great on small laptop screens: you get more room for every app.

Heads up: On Windows, turn on 'Automatically hide the taskbar' in Settings > Personalization > Taskbar.
