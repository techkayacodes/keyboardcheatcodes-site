Bookmark the page

Saves the current page to your bookmarks.

WindowsCtrl+D
Mac⌘+D

How to use it

  1. Press the shortcut.
  2. Pick a folder, or rename it.
  3. Press Enter to save.

Pro tip

Ctrl + Shift + B (⌘ + ⇧ + B) shows or hides the bookmarks bar.
