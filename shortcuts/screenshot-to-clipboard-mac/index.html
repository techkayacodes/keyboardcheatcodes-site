Copy a screenshot instead of saving it

On a Mac, add Control to any screenshot shortcut and it goes to the clipboard instead of becoming a file on your desktop.

WindowsNo built-in shortcut
Mac⌃+⌘+⇧+4

How to use it

  1. Press ⌃ + ⌘ + ⇧ + 4 and drag a box (or ⌃ + ⌘ + ⇧ + 3 for the full screen).
  2. Nothing is saved to the desktop.
  3. Paste it straight into an email, chat or document with ⌘ + V.

Pro tip

Perfect for sharing quick screenshots in Slack or Messages without cluttering your desktop.

Heads up: Windows' Win + Shift + S already copies to the clipboard by default.
